Programs

Practical help, from first resume to first paycheque.

Four kinds of support, mixed and matched around what you actually need. Nothing off a shelf, nothing you have to figure out alone.

Skills training

Short, hands on courses that build the skills Edmonton employers ask for, from digital basics and workplace communication to training for specific industries.

Employment support

Resumes, cover letters, interview practice and job search coaching, one on one, until the offer letter lands in your inbox.

Mentorship

Get matched with someone who has done the job before. Mentors tell you what the work is really like and open doors that job boards cannot.

Community connections

A new city is easier with people in it. Workshops, networking sessions and community events help you build a local network that lasts.

About UpChances

People over process, always.

UpChances runs on a simple belief: people grow faster than paperwork. Our core philosophy has always been people over process. We would rather sit down with you, hear your story, and build a plan around your life than hand you a stack of forms and wish you luck.

That approach keeps the work flexible and human. It also works. Employers across Edmonton trust the people we send their way because we know them as people first, not as files in a system.

We are a small Edmonton team of employment counsellors, trainers and connectors. Many of us came to Canada ourselves, or watched family members start over here. We know the first job is the hardest one to land, and we treat it that way.

Local first

We work with Edmonton employers every week, so the advice you get is about this job market, not a generic one.

Experience counts

Work you did before arriving in Canada is real experience. We help you present it so local employers see it too.

No one is a file number

You will talk to the same small team from your first chat to your first day at work.
